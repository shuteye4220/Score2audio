Better timing, an installer that sets everything up, automatic updates, videos, MP3 files and lyrics as text.

**Windows users:** from this version, download `score2audio-windows-setup.exe` and run it. It installs score2audio, and downloads and installs Audiveris and FFmpeg for you. Your scores and settings are kept. If you used the zip before, you can delete that folder afterwards. From now on score2audio updates itself.

### Timing
Testers found that changes of time signature were missed and that some pieces played at double or half speed. score2audio was tested on 20 public-domain scores (folk tunes, hymns, Bach, Handel, Mozart, Beethoven and more), printed in five music fonts, some made to look scanned, and compared with the exact timing:
- Printed metronome marks ("♩ = 72", "𝅗𝅥 = 80", "♩. = 54", "♪ = 70") are now read, including which note counts the beat. Audiveris leaves them out, so score2audio reads them from the page itself. All 12 marks in the tests were read correctly; before, none were.
- Where Audiveris misses or misreads a time signature, the measure lengths follow the notes: 312 of 344 test measures now have the right length, up from 243. The score's warnings say how many measures were corrected this way.
- A tempo word without a number ("Andante", "Slowly", "Presto") gives a usual tempo for that word. With no marking at all, the time signature decides, so music in 2/2 or 4/2 no longer starts at half speed.
- A misread tempo mark (such as "whole note = 4") is ignored.
- Lyrics and labels are now read even when another copy of Tesseract is installed on the computer.

### New
- **Installer for Windows**, which installs Audiveris and FFmpeg too (each download checked against its known fingerprint). Uninstall from Apps in Settings.
- **Automatic updates**, on Windows, Mac and Linux. In the Setup section, under Updates: install new versions automatically (the default), ask first, or do not check. Updates are installed only if they carry the project's signature.
- **Setup for Mac and Linux:** `Install helpers.command` on a Mac, `./setup.sh` on Linux, and an "Install the helper programs for me" button in the Setup section on Windows and Mac.
- **Save as video:** an MP4 video of the chosen lines as scrolling sheet music with the lyrics, and their sound, to send to others.
- **MP3 audio files**, when FFmpeg is installed: choose the audio file type next to "Save as audio file".
- With FFmpeg installed, videos from any browser get sound that plays everywhere.
- **Save lyrics as text:** all the lyrics found, verse by verse, one printed line per line, with measure numbers.
- **Linux on ARM** (for example a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 with a 64-bit system): `score2audio-linux-arm64.tar.gz`.

### Security and efficiency
- Audiveris, FFmpeg and updates are only ever downloaded from fixed addresses, and each is checked byte for byte before it is run, unpacked or installed; an update must also carry the project's signature.
- Updates only replace score2audio's own files, and never go backwards to an older version.
- The installer needs no administrator rights for score2audio itself.
